## 1. Geometric Motivation: From Continuous $E_7$ to Discrete Flavor Symmetry

### 1.1 The Flavor Problem in Unified Frameworks
The origin of fermion generations and their mixing patterns remains one of the least understood aspects of the Standard Model. Conventional approaches introduce ad-hoc flavon fields, assume spontaneous breaking of continuous flavor groups $SU(3)_F$, or rely on phenomenological texture zeros. While successful in fitting data, these methods lack a fundamental geometric or topological origin, and typically require multiple free parameters to reproduce the observed PMNS matrix.

In the **Symmetry–Phase–Unification (SPU)** framework, flavor is not postulated but emerges from the interplay between the compact vacuum geometry $E_7/SU(8)$, the topological structure of the fermionic condensate, and the spectral weighting of collective modes.

1.  **Finite capacity**: The Hilbert space of collective excitations is finite-dimensional, precluding continuous flavor rotations.
2.  **Spectral truncation**: The renormalization-group fixed point $\delta_* \approx 0.633$ introduces a non-uniform weighting $w(\lambda) \propto (1-\delta_*)$ across the Laplacian spectrum.
3.  **Topological boundary conditions**: The $n=3$ vortex in the condensate imposes twisted periodicity $\Phi(r,\theta+2\pi) = \Phi(r,\theta)$, which acts as a discrete cyclic permutation on the zero-mode subspace.

Harmonic analysis on compact symmetric spaces guarantees that the stabilizer of the vacuum configuration is a finite subgroup of the automorphism group of $M$. Consequently, $SU(3)_F$ is projected onto a discrete group $G_F \subset \text{Aut}(M)$.

### 1.3 Topological Origin of the $Z_3$ Flavor Index
The three fermion generations arise as **Jackiw–Rossi zero modes** bound to a vortex with winding number $n=3$:

$$\Phi(r,\theta) = \Delta(r) \, e^{i 3 \theta}, \quad \psi_\ell(r,\theta) \sim e^{i(\ell + 1/2)\theta} f_\ell(r), \quad \ell = 0,1,2.$$

The angular coordinate $\theta \in [0,2\pi)$ is compact, and the vortex winding enforces a residual $Z_3$ symmetry: $\theta \mapsto \theta + 2\pi/3$. This transformation permutes the zero-mode indices cyclically:

$$P_{Z_3}: \quad (\psi_0, \psi_1, \psi_2) \mapsto (\psi_1, \psi_2, \psi_0).$$

Thus, the flavor index $\ell$ is a topological quantum number associated with the angular momentum quantization in the vortex background.

### 1.4 Spectral Suppression and Emergent Discrete Symmetry
The dynamical parameter $\delta(\mu)$ flows to an infrared fixed point $\delta_* \approx 0.633$, yielding an effective capacity $N_f^{\text{eff}} \approx 127.4$. The vacuum expectation value of the neutral collective sector breaks the full automorphism group down to a discrete stabilizer $G_F$ that commutes with the spectral projector:

$$G_F = \{ g \in \text{Aut}(M) \mid g \, \Pi_{\text{IR}} = \Pi_{\text{IR}} \, g \}$$
