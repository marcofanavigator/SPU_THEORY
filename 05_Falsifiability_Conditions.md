# Falsifiability Conditions for SPU

## Abstract
A scientific theory must make predictions that can be experimentally tested and potentially falsified. This document lists the specific, testable predictions of the SPU framework and the conditions under which the theory would be considered falsified.

## 1. Core Predictions of SPU

### 1.1 Gauge Unification Predictions
- **Unification scale**: $M_{\mathrm{GUT}} \approx (1-2) \times 10^{16}$ GeV
- **Unification without SUSY**: Gauge couplings must unify without supersymmetric particles
- **Proton decay**: $\tau_p \sim 10^{34}\text{--}10^{35}$ years with specific decay channels
- **No new gauge bosons**: No detection of X/Y bosons below $M_{\mathrm{GUT}}$

### 1.2 Gravitational Predictions  
- **No running of $G_N$**: Newton's constant must not show RG running
- **Emergent Planck scale**: $M_{\mathrm{Pl}} \approx \sqrt{N_f^{\mathrm{eff}}} \times M_{\mathrm{GUT}}$
- **Tensor modes**: Primordial tensor-to-scalar ratio $r \sim 0.01\text{--}0.05$
- **No quantum gravity effects**: No deviations from classical GR at accessible energies

### 1.3 Cosmological Predictions
- **Dark energy equation of state**: $w \approx -1$ with possible mild evolution
- **Non-extensive vacuum energy**: $\rho_\Lambda \propto H^2 M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2$ not $M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^4$
- **Consistency with $\Lambda$CDM**: SPU must recover standard cosmology at late times

### 1.4 Galactic Dynamics Predictions
- **Rotation curves**: $v^2(r) = \frac{G_N M_b(r)}{r} \sqrt{1 + (r/r_t)^2}$
- **Tully-Fisher relation**: $V_f^4 \propto G M_b a_0$ with $a_0 \sim 10^{-10}$ m/s²
- **Universal acceleration scale**: Emergent from SPU dynamics
- **No dark matter**: Galactic dynamics explained without non-baryonic matter

## 2. Specific Falsification Conditions

### 2.1 Immediate Falsification (Single Measurement)
SPU would be falsified if any of the following are observed:
1. **Proton decay** with $\tau_p < 10^{33}$ years or $\tau_p > 10^{36}$ years
2. **Running of Newton's constant** $|dG_N/d\ln\mu| > 10^{-5}$ at any scale
3. **Detection of supersymmetric particles** below $10^{10}$ GeV
4. **Extra spatial dimensions** with size $R > 10^{-19}$ m
5. **Deviation from equivalence principle** $\Delta a/a > 10^{-15}$

### 2.2 Statistical Falsification (Multiple Measurements)
SPU would be statistically disfavored if:
1. **Gauge couplings** fail to unify within 5% of predicted $M_{\mathrm{GUT}}$
2. **CMB tensor modes** show $r < 0.001$ or $r > 0.1$
3. **Dark energy** shows $|w+1| > 0.1$ at any redshift
4. **Galactic rotation curves** require dark matter halo with specific density profile
5. **Higgs boson** shows compositeness scale $\Lambda_H < 10^{10}$ GeV

### 2.3 Theoretical Consistency Falsification
SPU would require major revision if:
1. **Value of $\delta$** needed for unification differs from $0.63 \pm 0.1$
2. **Gravitational scale** emerges at energy $\neq (1 \pm 0.5) \times 10^{17}$ GeV
3. **Three families** cannot be derived from topological index $n=3$
4. **Vacuum energy** calculation requires fine-tuning of $\delta$

## 5. Bayesian Evidence Framework
A quantitative falsification framework:
1. **Prior probability**: $P(\mathrm{SPU}) = 0.01$ (conservative)
2. **Likelihood**: $P(\mathrm{data}|\mathrm{SPU})$ from predictions
3. **Posterior**: Update based on experimental results
4. **Model comparison**: Bayes factor vs alternatives

SPU would be strongly disfavored (Bayes factor $< 0.01$) if:
- More than 3 major predictions fail at $>5\sigma$
- All predictions are less accurate than simpler models
- Required parameter tuning exceeds 1 part in $10^3$

## 6. Conclusion
SPU makes numerous specific, quantitative predictions across particle physics, cosmology, and astrophysics. The theory is highly falsifiable and will be tested by multiple experiments in the coming decades. This falsifiability is a strength, not a weakness, distinguishing SPU from less predictive approaches.

The future of SPU depends entirely on experimental data. Either the predictions will be confirmed, making SPU a serious candidate for a fundamental theory, or they will be refuted, requiring the framework to be abandoned or significantly modified.
