# Appendix A: Origin of the Number of Modes N=64 from Internal Symmetries of the Gravitational Medium

## A.1 Minimal Hypotheses (Explicit)

We assume only the following:

1. The gravitational medium is composed of fermionic degrees of freedom.
2. Gravitational response emerges from collective modes of the medium, not from isolated particles.
3. The medium is:
   - isotropic in space,
   - locally homogeneous,
   - devoid of privileged directions.

We do NOT assume:
- supersymmetry,
- strings,
- extra dimensions.

## A.2 Structure of Fermionic Degrees of Freedom

A minimal relativistic fermion possesses:

- 2 spin states ($\uparrow, \downarrow$)
- 2 effective dynamic polarizations (which here do not coincide with field chirality, but with medium response states)

**Minimum:**

$$2 \times 2 = 4$$

## A.3 Spatial Isotropy and Angular Decomposition

To avoid introducing privileged directions, the collective response must be decomposable into complete angular modes.

The minimal non-trivial set that:
- respects isotropy,
- allows constructive and destructive interference,
- permits saturation,

is a decomposition over eight equivalent directions (cube / octant):

$$N_{\text{ang}} = 8$$

This is the first value that:
- is not purely dipolar,
- does not break rotational symmetry on average.

## A.4 Phase Modes (Coherent / Decoherent)

Each angular mode can exist in two distinct macroscopic states:

- coherent phase (elastic response)
- decoherent phase (rigid or saturated response)

Therefore:

$$N_{\text{phase}} = 2$$

## A.5 Total Count of Modes

Multiplying the independent contributions:

$N_{\text{modes}} = (\text{spin}) \times (\text{polarization}) \times (\text{angular modes}) \times (\text{phase states})$

$= 2 \times 2 \times 8 \times 2$

$= \boxed{64}$

## A.6 Why Not 32 or 128?

### ❌ $N = 32$

Would require eliminating one of the factors above (typically the phase modes):

- no distinction between coherent / decoherent → impossible to describe a phase transition

Excluded phenomenologically (SPARC clearly shows transitions).

### ❌ $N = 128$

Would require doubling the angular modes:

- finer structure than necessary
- response too continuous
- loss of observed saturation

Excluded by SPARC data (which shows discrete plateaus, not continuous ones).

## A.7 Physical Interpretation

The number $N = 64$:

- is the minimum compatible with:
  - isotropy,
  - fermionicity,
  - phase transition,
  - finite collective response;

- introduces no ad-hoc symmetries;

- is not tuned to cosmological data.

In this sense, 64 plays a role analogous to:

- the number of degrees of freedom in a minimal elastic lattice,
- the number of phononic modes necessary for a three-dimensional solid response.

## A.8 Connection with Observational Results

It is remarkable that the same value:

- reproduces the observed saturation in SPARC galaxies,
- correctly predicts the amplitude of the Hubble tension,
- emerges from purely structural considerations of the medium.

This convergence suggests that the value $N = 64$ is not accidental, but reflects a fundamental property of the emerging gravitational system.
